In loving memory

Scout

This page is dedicated to my dog, Scout. He was a huge part of my life, even if he was just a dog, and I felt like he deserved a space to commemorate his life.

I got Scout at 2 months old, when he was a little tiny puppy. It did not take long to discover his love for playing fetch, particularly with a frisbee. He would spend countless hours running up and down my parents' back yard just chasing the frisbee, until he was beyond worn out.

Scout, you are sorely missed by everyone who had the pleasure of meeting you. You will live on through me forever, you helped me become the person I am today.

Rest well, scouty boy.
