September 20, 2014 – August 31, 2026

Scout may have been a dog, but he meant the world to me. I wanted a place where I, or anyone else, could come to experience some of what made Scout, Scout.

(The day we got him[01_scout_baby.jpg 27%])

Scout was born on September 20, 2014, in a little town east of Atlanta, Georgia. He came into my life just two months later, when my then-girlfriend and I decided to get a Golden Retriever. We chose the name Scout after watching Disney's Bears, released earlier that year, which featured a baby grizzly bear with the same name.

Raising a puppy from such a young age definitely came with its challenges, but looking back, I'm grateful I got to be there for nearly the beginning of his life. Even more than that, I'm grateful he got to be such a big part of mine.

From the moment Scout came home, I was never really alone. ([20150116_080135.jpg 21% print]) For nearly twelve years, he was my constant companion through the different chapters of my life. As he got older, he even joined me on multiple trips across the United States, sharing adventures I'll always remember.

It's hard to explain how much it hurts to know he's gone, but I take comfort in knowing that he lived a full life filled with frisbees, long walks, treats, adventures, and, above all, so much love.

Toward the end of August 2026, we discovered that Scout had cancer. We took him to the vet after he suddenly had difficulty walking. Tests showed some calcification in his spine, but we can't say conclusively whether it caused his sudden inability to walk. The vets also found many nodules in his lungs during an X-ray, which were concerning for cancer.

He regained the ability to walk a day or two later, but he began showing respiratory problems relatively soon afterward. Even then, the way he died did not seem like sudden respiratory failure; it appeared more like a stroke. We can't confirm exactly what happened. On August 31, 2026, he passed away rather suddenly, less than a month shy of his twelfth birthday.

I don't think you can ever truly prepare yourself for losing a companion who's been by your side for almost twelve years. Scout was there through so much of my life, and it's difficult to imagine what life will look like without him. I'll miss the little things, the familiar routines, and the memories I wish I could experience just one more time.

I still find myself looking at my plate after a meal and thinking, "Scout would really love to lick this plate." Of course, I can't give him any more plates to lick. No more long hugs just because, no more barking when he hears something outside. There are so many other little things I'll miss that I couldn't possibly list them all here.

I have so many beautiful memories with you, Scout. You weren't just my dog; you were my best friend, my adventure buddy, and my constant companion for nearly twelve years. You brought so much happiness into my life, and I hope you knew just how deeply you were loved.

I'll carry you with me for the rest of my life, Scout. You left a mark on my heart that time will never erase, and no matter how many years pass, you'll always be my good buddy.

Rest well, my Scouty boy. Thank you for nearly twelve wonderful years. I love you, and I'll miss you always.
